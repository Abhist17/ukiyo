An honest scoreboard for curated liquidity · a Uniswap v4 hook · Uniswap Hook Incubator, cohort 11

More and more of Uniswap's liquidity is run by curators who manage it for depositors. Depositors pick them by fee income or by “did they beat holding”, numbers that mostly measure the market, not the manager. So money follows the wrong curators. Ukiyo measures each curator's real skill inside the pool itself, with no oracle.

Edge = user fees + user price impact − arbitrage loss

What ordinary traders paid the curator, minus what arbitrageurs took. The market's direction is left out, so a bull run can't make a curator look skilled.

What's built and live

  • The hook keeps a ledger per curator on every swap, rounded against the curator wherever it has to choose.
  • Curator vaults that pay the curator only a share of Edge, with a bond that refunds depositors after a bad epoch.
  • A skill test on every vault (is it skill or luck?) and probation for a losing streak.
  • Open launches for any curator, and a router that splits a deposit by evidence of skill.
  • A developer kit: an SDK and ukiyo CLI that explains how any swap was booked, its maths matching the hook to the wei.
  • Live on Unichain Sepolia, with 113 tests, 5 stateful invariants and a threat model.

Who it's for

  • Depositors back skill instead of volume, and see whether a record is more than luck.
  • Curators get a track record nobody can dispute, and are paid for skill, not for trading volume.
  • Vault platforms rank and route on one on-chain call.

Honest limits

  • Testnet only and not yet audited. Next: an off-chain scoreboard for existing pools, a security review, a capped mainnet pilot.
  • Arbitrage is spotted by its priority fee; on today's Unichain flow that misses about 7% of informed trades, which the 5 bps pools Ukiyo targets tolerate.
